Faq.htm :: frequently asked_□×
What is NAV?

Net Asset Value — the risk-free treasury value divided by total supply, per token. It is the backing floor: the value you redeem against, and the reference point the entire dividend policy is measured from. It rises as bonds and buys add to reserves.

What is the premium, and why does it matter?

The premium P = market price / NAV — how much more the market pays than the backing. It is the only input to the dividend. At or below NAV the dividend is zero; at a 1.75× premium or higher it pays the full rate. The fund emits exactly the confidence the market extends to it.

How are dividends paid?

Enroll GRAHAM to receive sGRAHAM. Every 8-hour epoch the distribution compounds directly into your balance — nothing to claim. The rate follows R_MAX × clamp((P − 1)/(K − 1), 0, 1), with R_MAX 0.45% per epoch and K = 1.75.

Can I redeem at any time?

Yes. Redemption between sGRAHAM and GRAHAM is 1:1 and immediate at Shareholder Services — no lockups, no queues, no notice period.

How does the Bond Desk work?

Subscribe in USDC for GRAHAM notes priced at a discount to market with a hard floor at NAV. Because the floor is NAV, a bond can never sell GRAHAM below its existing backing — every subscription is accretive. Notes vest over two days.

What is Loopback?

The Lombard credit facility. Borrow USDC against enrolled sGRAHAM — up to 50% of collateral value — buy more GRAHAM, and enroll it, in one transaction. The simulator shows effective APY and a health/headroom meter. Leverage magnifies gains and losses alike.

What actually backs the token?

USDC reserves held in the treasury. The reserve cap means the distributor can never mint past the treasury's risk-free value — enforced every epoch by the contract, not an administrator.

Which chain, and how do I get USDC?

Graham lives on Base. Acquire USDC on Base through the Base app or by bridging, then connect an EVM wallet at Shareholder Services to purchase, enroll, bond, and loop.
